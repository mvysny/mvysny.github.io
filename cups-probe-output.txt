NOTE: $HOME/.cups/client.conf is tried FIRST by both libcups builds and
      there is no fallback if it opens. /etc/cups is the deb's; /var/snap
      is the snap's. SSLOptions must be in whichever one owns printing.

--- snap view (cups.lpstat)
    lpstat: No destinations added.

A proxy:// DeviceURI means that queue is a mirror of the deb's, made by
cups-proxyd. The real device URI - and the real TLS - lives on the deb.
